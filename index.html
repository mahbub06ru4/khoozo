What you can do with Khoozo

For requesters

  • Post a task in five quick steps: details, category, location, budget and photos
  • Your area is detected automatically, or type it in yourself
  • Choose how long offers stay open, from 30 minutes to 7 days
  • Compare offers with each helper's rating, then accept the ones you like
  • Search and track all your posts in one place

For providers & sellers

  • Browse tasks nearest-first, or sort by newest, lowest or highest budget
  • Filter by category, budget range or keyword
  • Send an offer before the timer runs out, and withdraw it any time
  • Ratings and reviews from every job build your reputation
  • Online Shop: subscribe (৳100/month or ৳1000/year) to list products for up to a year

Safety & trust

  • Every post is reviewed by our team before it goes live
  • ID verification (NID or birth certificate) at sign-up
  • A safety checklist before any contact details are shown
  • Phone numbers stay masked; talk on WhatsApp instead
  • Instant push and in-app notifications for every offer and decision

Khoozo Admin app

  • A moderation dashboard with live counts for every post status
  • Approve, reject with a reason, or schedule a post to go live later
  • Review ID documents for the requester and every provider who offered
  • Filter posts by status, category and date
  • Activate Online Shop seller subscriptions
  • Bangla & English
  • Light & dark mode
  • Browse without an account
  • Live Bangla ↔ English translation on any post
  • One account: post a task and offer help

About Khoozo

One brand. Four solutions, run with the same discipline.

Khoozo started as a software team and grew into three divisions — IT, Real Estate, and Travel — because the same question kept coming up: why does a software sprint get a clearer plan than a property investment or a visa application?

So every division works the same way: a documented scope, an honest timeline, and a single accountable contact from first consultation to delivery — whichever solution you came for.
