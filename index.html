About Solution 360

Built by an engineer.
Run like a consultancy should be.

Solution 360 Consultancy was founded on a simple observation: the industries people trust with life-changing decisions — where to study abroad, where to invest in property, how to build their next product — are too often run without the transparency and rigor that software teams take for granted.

We closed that gap. Every engagement — whether it's a mobile app sprint, a university application, or a land-share investment — follows the same disciplined process: clear scope, honest timelines, and a single point of accountability from first consultation to delivery.

Md. Mahbubur Rahman

Chairman & Founder, Solution 360 Consultancy

A technology enthusiast at heart and a Flutter developer by craft, Md. Mahbubur Rahman founded Solution 360 Consultancy on the belief that good consultancy is really just good engineering applied to people's biggest decisions. Over more than a decade building software for banking, logistics and consumer platforms, he saw the same pattern outside tech: travel plans, visa applications and property investments were too often handled with less rigor than a software sprint. Solution 360 was built to close that gap — bringing a builder's discipline to every consultation, whether it's shipping an app, booking a flight, or securing a share in Uttara's next development.
