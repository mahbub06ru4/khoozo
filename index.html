About Khoozo

Built by developers.
Run like a marketplace should be.

Khoozo is a local task and service marketplace for Bangladesh. Post a small job — a vehicle wash, a home repair, a parcel delivery, a passport errand — and get offers from nearby people who can help. Or browse what others need and make an offer yourself. There's no "customer" or "provider" account type: you can post a task in the morning and offer to help with someone else's in the afternoon.

Khoozo also runs an Online Shop, where subscribed sellers list products year-round in a storefront-style layout. Every member signs up with a government ID, every post is reviewed by our team before it goes live, and phone numbers stay masked until both sides accept an offer and acknowledge our safety guidance.
