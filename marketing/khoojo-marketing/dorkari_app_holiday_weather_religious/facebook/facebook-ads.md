# Dorkari (দরকারি) — Facebook Sponsored Posts (বাংলা + English)

A free, ad-free daily-essentials app: prayer times with Sehri/Iftar countdown, public-holiday calendar with reminders, Quran with translation and recitation, weather & air quality, news, live TV and exchange rates.

**How to use:** each post has a Headline (title), a Description, Primary text in Bangla and English, and its own hashtags. Copy each grey box as it is. Replace `[PLAY_STORE_LINK]` with your tracking link from `README.md`.

**Before boosting**
- The app must be live on Google Play first. Android only for now.
- Meta policy: never imply you know the viewer's religion ("Are you Muslim?") and don't target by religion. These posts only describe features.
- No download counts, fake reviews or "best / No. 1" claims. Don't print exact Ramadan dates (they depend on the moon sighting).
- Creatives: `creatives/fb_square_1080x1080.png`, `creatives/fb_story_1080x1920.png`, `creatives/fb_feed_1080x1350.png` (Ramadan), `creatives/fb_link_1200x628.png` (link ads).
