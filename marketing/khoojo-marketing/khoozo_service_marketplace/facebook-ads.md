# Khoozo App (খুঁজো অ্যাপ) — Facebook Sponsored Posts (বাংলা + English)

A location-based marketplace: people post a task or service request, nearby providers send offers, and the customer hires with confidence. Web app, admin panel and Flutter mobile app; Bangla & English.

**How to use:** each post has a Headline (title), a Description, Primary text in Bangla and English, and its own hashtags. Copy each grey box as it is.

**Before boosting**
- App link: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.khoozo.app (add `&referrer=utm_source%3Dfacebook%26utm_medium%3Dpaid%26utm_campaign%3Dlaunch` to track installs).
- The service examples (AC repair, shifting, tutor…) are examples of what users can post. Remove any category the app doesn't support yet.
- The posts mention in-app chat, job tracking and reviews. Delete any of those lines the current app version doesn't have.
- Don't claim "verified" or "background-checked" providers unless that check really happens.

Contact used in all posts: 📞 / WhatsApp 01818-120900 · 🌐 www.khoozo.com
