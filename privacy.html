Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Khoozo ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information across our website, the Khoozo marketplace app, and our four solution divisions — IT, Real Estate, Visa Solutions, and Travel & Holiday.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you give us directly — name, phone number, email — when you fill out our contact form, book a consultation, or sign up in the Khoozo app.
  • Engagement details relevant to your chosen division (e.g. travel dates for Travel & Holiday, intended study destination for Visa Solutions, property interest for Real Estate, project brief for IT).
  • App account data, for users of the Khoozo app — a government ID document for identity verification, posts, offers, messages and reviews, as described in the app's own sign-up flow and in-app privacy notice.
  • Technical data such as browser type, pages visited and device information, collected automatically for security and to improve the site.

3. How We Use It

  • To respond to your enquiry and deliver the specific division's service you requested.
  • To verify identity and reduce fraud for Khoozo app accounts and for KYC-sensitive services (e.g. visa, real estate).
  • To send updates about an active engagement — never unsolicited marketing without your consent.
  • To improve our website and services.

4. What We Don't Do

We don't sell your personal data to third parties. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (e.g. hosting, email delivery), the specific partner needed to complete your engagement (e.g. an airline for ticketing, a university for an application), or when required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No online system is 100% secure, but we review our practices as Khoozo grows.

6. Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you by contacting us below. Khoozo app users can also manage their profile and account directly in the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults making their own IT, real estate, visa and travel decisions. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Khoozo evolves. Continued use of our website, app or services after a change means you accept the update.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support.khoozo@gmail.com or call 01818-120900.
