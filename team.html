Md. Mahbubur Rahman

Chairman & Founder, Khoozo

A technology enthusiast at heart and a Flutter developer by craft, Md. Mahbubur Rahman founded Khoozo on the belief that good consultancy is really just good engineering applied to people's biggest decisions. Over more than a decade building software for banking, logistics and consumer platforms, he saw the same pattern outside tech: travel plans, visa applications and property investments were too often handled with less rigor than a software sprint. Khoozo was built to close that gap — bringing a builder's discipline to every consultation, whether it's shipping an app, booking a flight, or securing a share in Uttara's next development.

"We don't sell services. We build outcomes — the same way we'd build a product."
